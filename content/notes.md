## offtakers: Announced offtakers
- Offtaker details are sourced from announced public deals only and are therefore non-exhaustive.
- When one announcement gives a single volume for several named buyers and no split, each buyer is credited an equal share.
- All Regions charts: volumes are as stated in each announcement (often multi-year totals); deals without a stated volume are listed but not counted. "Already issuing" means the deal's project has issued credits in its registry; other deals count as announced.

## countries: Country
- Asia excludes mainland China, North Korea and the Middle East. North America includes Central America and the Caribbean. Europe includes Turkey, the Caucasus and Russia; Africa covers all African countries.
- Multi-country projects appear once in the table, and their credit volumes are pro-rated according to country population.

## registries: Registry
- One project may be cross-registered and is then merged into one row. Merging based on closely matched project names may be subject to a degree of inaccuracy.
- Issued is summed across registries; a vintage year issued in two registries counts once.

## stages: Stage
- Listed: listed, under development or under validation.
- Validated: registered, nothing issued yet.
- Issuing: at least one issuance.
- Inactive: withdrawn, rejected, deregistered, or crediting period ended with no issuance (hidden by default).
- Announced deal: public purchase announcement with no single registry record.

## years: Years
- Project timelines are as published by the registry, where available.

## estimate: Est. annual volume
- Estimated by the registry, which is often higher than the volume later issued.
- Map totals leave out projects whose crediting period has ended, and (by default) inactive projects.

## units: Issued and retired volume
- Issued and retired volumes are cumulative.
- "Retired by" (in a project's details) names the retirement beneficiaries in the registry's own transaction records, as compiled by [CarbonPlan OffsetsDB](https://carbonplan.org/research/offsetsdb) (Verra, Gold Standard, ACR, CAR, ART TREES). Many retirements name no beneficiary; those are shown as "unnamed".

## delivery: % issued and % retired
- **% issued** = credits issued so far ÷ (registry estimate per year × crediting years to date). Years run from the earliest of crediting start, registration and first vintage to the latest issued vintage or two years ago, whichever is later, because registries issue one to two years after the vintage; capped at the crediting end, and at 2020 for CDM-only projects. Shown in **pink** when under 50%, i.e. the project is issuing well below its estimate.
- **% retired** = credits retired ÷ credits issued. The rest is still outstanding (unsold or unclaimed).

## eligibility: Eligibility
- **ICVCM CCP-approved methodology**: the project uses a methodology that the [ICVCM](https://icvcm.org/assessment-status/) has approved under a CCP-Eligible programme (Verra, Gold Standard, ACR, CAR, Isometric). ICVCM approves specific versions, and the CCP label itself is put on individual credits at issuance, so check the registry for CCP-labelled credits.
- **Article 6** labels come from the [UNEP-CCC Article 6 Pipeline](https://a6pipeline.unepccc.org/); "authorized" = registered or units issued there. Corresponding adjustments are not published per project in a machine-readable form, so they are not labelled.
- **On hold / under registry review**: as published in the registry's project status.
- CORSIA eligibility is not labelled yet: it is decided per credit (host-country authorisation and the registry's CORSIA label), and no public per-project list has been sourced.

## tags: Tag
- Tags are assigned only from what the registry record itself states (project name, methodology, registry project type, and the registry's published project description for Gold Standard and Verra; small hydro also from stated capacity ≤ 25 MW), so a project whose record does not mention a practice is not tagged with it. Exception: every rice-methane project is tagged AWD, the core practice in practically all of them.
- Search a tag or its alias (e.g. AWD, ERW, enhanced rock weathering) to see only projects with that tag.
